# Clause: Referral (Capped)

Optional clause for §09 Partnership Terms — use only where the client brief
or manifest records a referral arrangement. Not included by default.

---

Where the Client refers a new customer to SGC TECH AI who subsequently
signs and completes mobilisation on a paid engagement, SGC TECH AI will
credit the Client a referral amount equal to five percent (5%) of that new
customer's initial implementation fee (excluding recurring subscription,
hosting, and support fees), applied as a credit against the Client's own
recurring subscription fees.

This referral credit is capped at a maximum of **two (2) referral credits
per twelve (12)-month period**, and at a maximum aggregate credit of
**one (1) month's subscription fee** per twelve (12)-month period,
regardless of the number of qualifying referrals in excess of the cap.
Referral credits have no cash value and are not transferable.
