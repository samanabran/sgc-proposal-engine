# Clause: Referral (Capped)

**Purpose**: offer a bounded, self-funding referral incentive without the
uncapped exposure `failure-modes/known-defects.md` #13 documents.

**requires_counsel_review**: false.

**When mandatory**: optional — only when a referral arrangement is
discussed and logged in the client's brief.

**When it must NOT be used**: never leave the cap or the "paid and
collected" condition out — an uncapped or pre-collection referral credit
is an open liability, not a marketing incentive.

---

## Approved verbatim text

> One month's subscription credit is applied for each referred client who
> becomes a paying subscriber, capped at two credits per twelve-month
> period, and applied only after the referred client's first invoice has
> been paid and collected in full.
