# Clause: Key Person and Subcontractor

**Purpose**: reserve a substitution right so no engagement depends on a
single named consultant (G27) — closes the single-point-of-failure gap
`failure-modes/known-defects.md` #17 documents.

**requires_counsel_review**: true.

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW.** Do not include in an issued proposal until
> reviewed by UAE counsel, particularly if the Client has separately been
> given any verbal assurance naming a specific consultant.

**When mandatory**: every proposal.

**When it must NOT be used**: never promise a named individual consultant
as a guarantee, in this clause or anywhere else in the proposal or in
sales conversation.

---

## Draft text (pending counsel review)

> SGC TECH AI assigns qualified personnel to this engagement and reserves
> the right to substitute personnel, including subcontracted specialists,
> at its discretion, provided the substitution does not materially reduce
> the quality of delivery under this agreement.
