# Clause: Data Portability

**Purpose**: state the absolute, unconditional client-data export right
(G18) — this is the one clause that never bends, even during a dispute
or suspension.

**requires_counsel_review**: false.

**When mandatory**: every proposal.

**When it must NOT be used**: never qualify this with "subject to full
payment" or any other condition — see
`risk-security-matrix.yaml: absolute_rule`.

---

## Approved verbatim text

> The Client's data is exportable in full, on request, at any time —
> including during any dispute or period of suspended service access.
> This right is unconditional and is not affected by the payment or
> security terms elsewhere in this agreement.
