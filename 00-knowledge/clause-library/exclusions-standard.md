# Clause: Standard Exclusions

Use verbatim (extend, never trim, without Commercial Desk sign-off) in §07
Options & Inclusions / §08 Implementation & Recovery. Every proposal
includes exclusions — Commercial Rule 10. Append vertical-specific
exclusions from the relevant `market-data/vertical-notes/` file rather than
editing the list below in place.

---

The following are excluded from the scope and pricing of this proposal
unless explicitly listed as an included module, work package, or option
elsewhere in this document:

- Third-party software licensing costs not explicitly listed in this
  proposal's commercial terms.
- Custom development or configuration beyond the work packages listed in
  §06 Solution (Phase 1) and §07 Options & Inclusions.
- Data migration from source systems not identified in the Client's brief
  at the time of this proposal, or migration volumes materially exceeding
  the figures stated in the Client's brief.
- Ongoing change management, internal communications, or user adoption
  activity beyond the training sessions included in this proposal (see
  `adoption.md`).
- Hardware procurement, on-premise infrastructure, or network setup at the
  Client's premises.
- Regulatory, tax, or legal advisory services. Where this proposal
  references compliance requirements (VAT, RERA/DLD, ZATCA, or similar),
  such references are operational configuration guidance only and do not
  constitute legal or tax advice — see the applicable vertical note.
- Integration with third-party systems not explicitly named in this
  proposal's scope.
- Support and maintenance beyond the tier and term specified in §11
  Support & SLA.
- Travel and accommodation beyond what is explicitly itemized, where
  onsite work is included.

Any work requested outside this list is treated as a change request and
quoted separately, at the rates current in SGC TECH AI's rate card at the
time of the request.
