# Clause: Post-Recovery Continuation

**Purpose**: state clearly that the subscription rate does not drop once
the build is fully recovered (G5) — the recurring fee was never just a
financing vehicle, it also covers ongoing cost-to-serve.

**requires_counsel_review**: false.

**When mandatory**: every subscription proposal with a defined recovery
period.

**When it must NOT be used**: never write or imply a fee reduction after
recovery in any other section of the proposal — see
`failure-modes/known-defects.md` #7 for what happens when this is
contradicted elsewhere in the document.

---

## Approved verbatim text

> The implementation component is fully recovered by the end of the
> committed term. Thereafter the subscription continues month-to-month at
> the same rate with 30 days' written notice. No reduction in the
> subscription fee is implied.
