# Clause: Post-Recovery Continuation

Use verbatim in §09 Partnership Terms whenever G2 (Term ≥ recovery,
`commercial-rules/subscription-guardrails.md`) confirms the initial term
fully recovers mobilisation and build cost. This clause governs what
happens to pricing once that recovery point has passed.

---

Once the implementation investment referenced in this agreement's
financing structure has been fully recovered through the initial term (as
established at the time of this proposal per the applicable pricing
worksheet), the recurring subscription fee for any renewal term will be
recalculated to remove the financing uplift described in
`financing-disclosure.md`, reflecting the ongoing cost of licensing,
hosting, and support alone. SGC TECH AI will notify the Client in writing
of the adjusted fee no less than thirty (30) days before the renewal term
begins.

This adjustment applies only to the financing uplift component. Standard
annual price adjustments for licensing, hosting, and support, if any, are
governed separately and communicated on the same notice schedule.

legal sign-off.**
