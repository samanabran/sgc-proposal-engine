# Clause: Suspension and Reinstatement

**Purpose**: state the self-help suspension right on non-payment
(`risk-security-matrix.yaml: instrument_types.self_help`) while
guaranteeing data export remains unconditional (G18).

**requires_counsel_review**: true.

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW.** Do not include in an issued proposal until
> reviewed by UAE counsel. Notice-period adequacy and reinstatement terms
> should be checked against current UAE commercial practice.

**When mandatory**: any proposal carrying a deferred or recurring
payment obligation (i.e. essentially all subscription proposals).

**When it must NOT be used**: never state or imply suspension extends to
withholding the Client's data — data export is unconditional under any
circumstance, including active suspension (G18).

## Draft text (pending counsel review)

> SGC TECH AI may suspend service access, with 14 days' written notice, if
