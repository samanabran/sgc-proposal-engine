# Clause: Price Lock

**Purpose**: guarantee the subscription rate is fixed for the committed
term — distinct from `post-recovery-continuation.md`, which governs what
happens *after* the term.

**requires_counsel_review**: false.

**When mandatory**: every subscription proposal.

**When it must NOT be used**: never combine with language suggesting the
rate could rise mid-term for reasons other than a client-requested scope
change (a change request is priced separately, not a rate change).

---

## Approved verbatim text

> The subscription fee quoted in this proposal is fixed for the full
> committed term and will not increase during that period, except where
> the Client requests additional scope, which is quoted and agreed
> separately as a change request.
