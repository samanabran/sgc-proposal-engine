# Clause: Post-Dated Cheques

**Purpose**: document post-dated cheque (PDC) security where the risk
band requires `pdc_set` or `pdc_full_balance` (`risk-security-matrix.yaml`).

**requires_counsel_review**: true.

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW.** Do not include in an issued proposal until
> reviewed by UAE counsel. Enforceability of cheque-based security and the
> criminal-liability regime around dishonoured cheques in the UAE varies
> by emirate and by recent legal reform — verify current status before use.

**When mandatory**: `elevated` risk band and above per
`risk-security-matrix.yaml`, or `deferred_start` cadence structures.

**When it must NOT be used**: never present as the only security
instrument at `high` risk band — combine with mobilisation per the band's
full instrument list.

---

## Draft text (pending counsel review)

> The Client provides a set of post-dated cheques covering [the deferred
> balance / the full contract value], to be presented only in the event of
> non-payment under the terms of this agreement.
