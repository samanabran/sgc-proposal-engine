# Clause: Force Majeure and Third-Party Dependency

**Purpose**: cover events outside SGC's control, including third-party
dependencies this specific business relies on heavily — portal API access
(Property Finder, Bayut, Dubizzle), RERA/DLD systems, and Odoo's own
open-source release cadence for Community-edition upgrades.

**requires_counsel_review**: true.

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW.** Do not include in an issued proposal until
> reviewed by UAE counsel.

**When mandatory**: every proposal with portal integration or other
named third-party dependency in scope.

**When it must NOT be used**: never use this clause to disclaim
responsibility for delays within SGC's own control.

---

## Draft text (pending counsel review)

> Neither party is liable for delay or failure to perform caused by
> circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including third-party
> system outages, changes to third-party portal or regulatory system
> requirements (e.g. RERA/DLD, property portal APIs), or force majeure
> events. Where a third-party dependency identified in this proposal's
> assumptions is delayed or unavailable, affected milestones and any
> associated service-credit guarantees are extended or suspended
> accordingly.
