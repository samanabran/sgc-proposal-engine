# Proposal Section Map (v2)

Required contents of each proposal section, §01–§13. Every drafted
proposal contains all thirteen, in this order. This map is the checklist
a reviewer uses at `04-review` — a thin section is a review-log finding,
not a pass.

| § | File | Required contents | Primary source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | `01-executive-summary.md` | Outcome, scope snapshot, headline commercial figure, edition stated | `02-calc/pricing-worksheet.yaml`, client brief |
| 02 | `02-about.md` | SGC positioning | `market-data/benchmarks.yaml` |
| 03 | `03-understanding-business.md` | Client context, vertical, decision maker | `00-intake/client-brief.yaml` |
| 04 | `04-as-is.md` | Current-state, incumbent system | Client brief, vertical notes |
| 05 | `05-to-be.md` | Target-state, phased approach | Client brief, runbook |
| 06 | `06-solution-phase1.md` | Work packages, deliverables, **edition stated with capability exclusions if Community (G38)** | `pricing-worksheet.yaml: number_2_build`, `hour-lookup.yaml`, `clause-library/edition-and-upgrades.md` |
| 07 | `07-options-inclusions.md` | Phase 2 options, assumptions, standard exclusions | `phase2-catalogue.yaml`, `clause-library/exclusions-standard.md` |
| 08 | `08-implementation-recovery.md` | Timeline, milestones, incumbent replacement | `clause-library/exclusivity-replacement.md` |
| 09 | `09-partnership-terms.md` | Term, adoption, clawback, referral, key-person, IP, force majeure, dispute clauses as applicable | `clause-library/*` |
| 10 | `10-commercial-terms.md` | Pricing table (two options, never three), VAT (no charge), financing disclosure, cadence, exposure summary | `pricing-worksheet.yaml: assembly`, `clause-library/vat-uae.md`, `vat-gross-up.md`, `financing-disclosure.md`, `payment-cadence.md` |
| 11 | `11-support-sla.md` | Support tier, service credit guarantee table, training | `support-training.yaml`, `service-credit-guarantee.md` |
| 12 | `12-why-sgc.md` | Differentiators | `03-library/worked-examples/` |
| 13 | `13-next-steps.md` | Approval, signature block | `04-governance/approval-matrix.md` |

Every commercial figure traces back to the worksheet. No section
introduces a number the worksheet doesn't contain, and no section may
contain any of the forbidden phrases listed in
`01-templates/qa/pre-send-checklist.template.md`.
