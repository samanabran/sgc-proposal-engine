Consequences of Termination — Recovery Balance Counsel review — Appendix 2

Where this Agreement is terminated before the end of the Initial Term for any reason other than SGC Tech AI's uncured material breach, the Recovery Balance becomes immediately due and payable as a debt. This reflects value already delivered to, and financed on behalf of, the Client — build work performed and paid for on the Client's behalf ahead of full settlement — and is drafted as a debt-recovery mechanism, not as a penalty for termination.

Intellectual Property Schedule

The underlying Odoo software is licensed under its own terms — LGPL-3 for Community components, or the Odoo Enterprise licence where the Edition is Enterprise. SGC Tech AI grants no rights of its own in Category A; the Client's rights are those granted by the applicable Odoo licence directly.

Category B — Client Configuration and Client Data

The Client owns Client Data unconditionally at all times. The Client's rights to Configuration are as stated in §A.2 and §D.3; export rights are governed by Part E.

Category C — SGC Reusable Modules

SGC Tech AI owns its reusable modules developed independent of any single engagement — including commission engine, tenancy, maintenance, reporting, AI orchestration, document extraction, and lead scoring modules ("Reusable Modules"). The Client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable licence to use the Reusable Modules solely as installed for the Client's own instance. This licence vests — becomes irrevocable — only upon full settlement of the Recovery Balance. Before full settlement, the licence is provisional and subject to §A.16 on termination.

Category D — SGC Background IP

SGC Tech AI owns absolutely all background intellectual property, including prompts, orchestration logic, and methods used to deliver the engagement, whether or not embedded in the Client's Configuration.

Liquidated Damages for Unauthorized Redistribution

Redistribution, resale, or sublicensing of Category C or Category D material without SGC Tech AI's prior written consent is subject to liquidated damages of 2× the annual Subscription Fee, reflecting the genuine pre-estimated loss such conduct causes to SGC Tech AI's reusable-module business, not a penalty.

Audit Right

SGC Tech AI may audit the Client's use of Category C and D material on reasonable notice, no more than once per Contract Year absent a reasonable belief of breach.
