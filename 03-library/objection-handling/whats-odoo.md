# Objection: "What's Odoo? Never heard of it."

## Why it comes up

Boutique brokerage owners are comparing SGC against named brands
(PropSpace, generic "CRM software") and a platform name they don't
recognize reads as risk, not as a feature.

## The response

Odoo is the world's most widely deployed open-source business platform —
not a small vendor's proprietary tool, a mature open-source project with
a large global implementation ecosystem. SGC TECH AI configures, hosts,
and supports it end to end; the client never needs to interact with
"Odoo" as a separate vendor. See `03-library/snippets/odoo-explained-plain.md`
for the plain-language explainer to send or read verbatim.

## Exact language

> "Odoo's the platform underneath — think of it the way you might think
> of a car's engine brand versus the dealership you actually deal with.
> We're the dealership: we build, host, and support everything. You'll
> never need to go looking for 'Odoo support' separately."
