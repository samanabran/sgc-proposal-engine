# What is Odoo? (Plain-language explanation)

For use when a client asks "what actually is this software you're
proposing?" No sales framing here — just clarity. Pair with
`differentiation-vs-crm.md` if the client is also comparing against a
point CRM tool.

---

Odoo is business management software. Instead of one program that does one
job, it's a set of connected modules that each handle a different part of
running a company — sales, accounting, inventory, HR, projects, customer
support, and more — and they all share the same underlying data.

That last part is the key difference from most business software: when a
salesperson creates a quote in Odoo, and the client accepts it, that quote
can become an invoice in Accounting without anyone re-typing the numbers.
When a warehouse team receives stock, Accounting sees the value on the
books automatically. One record, used by every department that needs it,
instead of the same information copied by hand into three different
systems.

A company doesn't have to use every module. Most clients start with two or
three — commonly Sales, CRM (tracking leads and deals), and Accounting —
and add more later as they need them (Inventory, HR, Projects, and so on).
Each module is priced and licensed separately, so the system grows with
the business rather than requiring a large upfront commitment to
functionality that isn't needed yet.

Odoo itself is built and maintained by a Belgian software company (also
called Odoo), used by companies worldwide across every size, from small
shops to large enterprises. SGC TECH AI is an implementation partner — the
team that sets it up, configures it to match how a specific business
actually works, migrates existing data into it, and trains the team to use
it. Odoo the software is the same everywhere; the implementation is what
makes it fit a particular company.
