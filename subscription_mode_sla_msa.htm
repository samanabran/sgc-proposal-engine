How to Use This Document

This is the client-facing Master Service Agreement (“MSA”) and Service Level Agreement (“SLA”) of Scholarix Global Consultant FZE, trading as SGC Tech AI. It is drafted specifically for the Subscription Recovery Model, in which no separate implementation invoice is issued and the Implementation Value is recovered inside the Platform Subscription across the Initial Term.

This edition supersedes v2026.06 in full. It exists because v2026.06 assumed milestone-billed implementation and therefore contradicted every subscription-model proposal it was sent alongside. That contradiction is resolved here structurally, not by amendment.

Do not mix editions

Never issue this document together with a milestone-billed proposal, and never issue v2026.06 together with a subscription proposal. One commercial model per engagement, stated on the Order Form, carried consistently through both documents. Appendix IV is the mandatory reconciliation check.

Fields you may edit

Only two kinds of field are editable. Fields shown as LIKE THIS are populated from the signed Order Form. Fields shown as RESOLVE are company facts that must be settled once, centrally, before this document is issued to any client — they are not per-client variables. No other clause may be altered except by dated addendum signed by the Founder & CEO.

Outstanding RESOLVE items — block issue until settled

1. Licence authority and number. IFZA and DIFC are different authorities with different regulatory meaning. State the true one.
2. Registered address. One address only. Prior documents stated two different addresses.
3. Tax registration status. SGC Tech AI is not currently VAT-registered and must not represent otherwise, nor charge VAT. See §6.4.
4. Insurance in force. §17.3 is a warranty. Verify cover exists before signature.
5. Upgrade policy election. Version-Pinned or Upgrade-Included. See §5.4.

1 · Structure, Precedence & Entire Agreement

1.1 This Agreement comprises the body of this MSA together with Schedule B (Service Levels), Schedule C (Fees), Schedule D (Intellectual Property & Licensing), Schedule E (Exit & Transition), Schedule F (AI-Specific Provisions), Schedule G (Real Estate Configuration Baseline), the Order Form (Appendix I), the Authorised Users Form (Appendix II) and the Consistency Map (Appendix IV), each incorporated by reference.

1.2 Precedence. Precedence is split by subject matter, which is the mechanism by which this edition prevents the contradiction that arose under v2026.06:

  1. Commercial figures — all monetary amounts, the Implementation Value, Mobilisation Fee, Recovery Component, Platform Component, Subscription Fee, billing cadence, Initial Term length, user counts, platform edition and upgrade policy election are governed exclusively by the Order Form. Where the Order Form and the MSA body differ on any such figure, the Order Form prevails.
  2. Legal terms — all other provisions, including liability, intellectual property, confidentiality, termination mechanics, dispute resolution and data protection, are governed by the body of this MSA and its Schedules. Where the Order Form purports to alter a legal term, the MSA body prevails unless the Order Form expressly identifies the clause being modified and is signed by an authorised signatory of each Party.
  3. Proposals and pre-contractual material — superseded, save that where a proposal issued to Client describes the commercial model, the Implementation Value, or any capability as included, and this Agreement or the Order Form is silent or inconsistent, the reconciliation recorded in Appendix IV governs and SGC Tech AI may not rely on that silence to Client's detriment.
Why 1.2(c) exists

Ordinary entire-agreement clauses let a supplier disclaim what its own proposal promised. That is commercially corrosive and, in a boutique market where reputation travels faster than contracts, worth more to SGC Tech AI as a stated commitment than as a reserved right. Appendix IV forces the reconciliation to happen before signature, when it is cheap.

1.3 Entire agreement. Subject to 1.2(c), this Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior proposals, negotiations and understandings, written or oral.

1.4 No reliance on unwritten promises. Any capability, integration or service described verbally during the sales process and not recorded in the Order Form or Part G is not included. Appendix IV requires each such item to be recorded as included, deferred and priced, or expressly excluded. An item omitted from Appendix IV altogether is deemed excluded.

3.1 Components. SGC Tech AI provides: (i) Implementation, recovered under §5 rather than separately invoiced; (ii) Platform Subscription, mandatory and recurring; (iii) Out-of-Scope Work, tiered hourly under Schedule C.4; and (iv) optional add-ons elected on the Order Form. 3.2 Standard of performance. SGC Tech AI shall perform the Services using commercially reasonable efforts and professional skill and care consistent with prevailing industry standards for providers of comparable size and scope. This Agreement creates no warranty of absolute, uninterrupted or error-free performance beyond the express service levels in Schedule B.

3.3 AI disclaimer. AI-powered features are provided on an “as-is” and “as-available” basis. SGC Tech AI does not warrant the accuracy, completeness, reliability or fitness for purpose of any AI Output. Client acknowledges AI models may produce incorrect, incomplete, fabricated or biased outputs and bears sole responsibility for independently verifying AI Output before relying on it. Full terms in Schedule F.

3.4 Acceptance. On completion of the Configuration Baseline SGC Tech AI shall issue written notice of completion. Client shall have ten Business Days to test against the acceptance criteria in the Order Form and either accept in writing or reject with a written statement of material deficiencies. Failure to respond within ten Business Days constitutes deemed acceptance. Where Client rejects with a valid statement of material deficiencies, SGC Tech AI shall have fifteen Business Days to cure, after which the deliverable is resubmitted under this same procedure.

Acceptance does not start the Term

Under v2026.06 acceptance triggered the Term. It no longer does. The Term runs from Kickoff (§4.1) because SGC Tech AI performs the majority of the Implementation effort in the first three weeks and must not carry that cost outside the contract period.

3.5 Change control. Any request to modify scope shall be documented in a written Change Order specifying the change, its impact on fees and timeline, and the resource tier required. No Change Order takes effect until signed by both Parties. Requests exceeding scope without a signed Change Order are Out-of-Scope Work.

4 · Term, Commencement & Renewal

4.1 Commencement. The Term commences at Kickoff and continues for the Initial Term stated in the Order Form. G6

4.2 Initial Term never shorter than Recovery Period. The Initial Term shall in no case be shorter than the Recovery Period. Where the Order Form states a Recovery Period of twenty-four months, the Initial Term is twenty-four months. G2

4.3 Renewal. This Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve-month terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty days before the end of the then-current Term. Failure to give timely notice constitutes acceptance of renewal at the pricing then in effect, subject to §16.

4.4 Position after the Recovery Period. On completion of the Recovery Period the Implementation Value is fully recovered. The Subscription Fee thereafter continues at the same rate. No reduction in the Subscription Fee is implied, and the composition of the Subscription Fee as between Recovery Component and Platform Component is an internal allocation which does not create any entitlement to a reduced fee. G5

Clause 4.4 replaces a defect

The predecessor commercial documents stated that after recovery the subscription would continue “consisting of the platform portion only — effectively a lower-cost running fee.” That committed SGC Tech AI to a permanently loss-making renewal year. Any proposal issued alongside this Agreement must use the §4.4 wording.

5 · The Subscription Recovery Model

This section is the commercial core of the Agreement. It replaces the milestone-billed implementation regime of v2026.06 in its entirety.

5.1 No separate implementation invoice. The Implementation Value stated in the Order Form is not invoiced as a separate fee. It is recovered inside the Subscription Fee through the Recovery Component across the Recovery Period. Save for the Mobilisation Fee, Out-of-Scope Work, elected add-ons and third-party pass-through costs, the Subscription Fee is the whole of what Client pays.

5.2 Mobilisation Fee. A Mobilisation Fee equal to the percentage of the Implementation Value stated in the Order Form, being not less than thirty-three percent (33%) unless expressly approved otherwise in writing by SGC Tech AI's Commercial Desk, is payable at Kickoff. The Mobilisation Fee is applied in reduction of the Implementation Value and is non-refundable once Kickoff has occurred, save on a termination for cause by Client under §15.3. G3 G34

5.3 Financing component and its removal. Where any part of the Implementation Value is recovered over time rather than paid at Kickoff, the deferred amount carries a financing component reflecting the Recovery Period, disclosed in the Order Form. Client may at any time settle the Recovery Balance in whole or in part, in which case the unaccrued financing component attributable to the amount settled is cancelled and the Recovery Component is recalculated for the remainder of the Recovery Period. G7

5.4 What the Recovery Component funds. The Recovery Component funds the Configuration Baseline in Part G as modified by the Order Form, together with data migration within the stated caps, documentation, quality assurance, project management and the training sessions stated in the Order Form. It does not fund Out-of-Scope Work, deferred-phase items, or version upgrades except where the Order Form elects the Upgrade-Included policy under §8.3.

5.5 Discount treatment — asymmetric and mandatory. Any prepayment discount, promotional adjustment or commercial concession applies to the Platform Component only. The Recovery Component represents work already performed and remunerated at cost by SGC Tech AI and is not discountable. The sole concession available in respect of the Recovery Component is reduction or removal of the financing component under §5.3. G11

Why 5.5 is drafted as a prohibition

A blanket “10% for annual prepayment” applied to the whole Subscription Fee gives away delivered work. On a representative boutique engagement it removes roughly one third of the total margin and can take the engagement below its cost of delivery. The asymmetry in 5.5 is economically correct — the client stops borrowing, so the client stops paying to borrow — and it is self-limiting, because the financing component is the only thing available to concede.

5.6 Recovery Balance is a debt. The Recovery Balance at any date represents the unrecovered value of Implementation work already performed. It is a debt for services rendered. It is not a forecast of future fees, not liquidated damages, and not a penalty. Its treatment on termination is set out in §15.4. G4 G16 COUNSEL

Forbidden wording

Neither this Agreement nor any proposal may state that no VAT applies because SGC Tech AI operates from a free zone — this is factually wrong, as Designated Zone treatment applies to goods and not services. Why this clause is here

The most common cause of churn in this segment is not price and not defect — it is a team that stops entering data. §10 is the cheapest retention instrument available and it converts the client's largest stated fear into a contractual commitment.

13 · Liability

13.1 Cap. Each Party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement in any rolling twelve-month period is capped at the total Subscription Fees paid by Client to SGC Tech AI during that period. COUNSEL

13.2 Exclusions. Neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential, special, punitive or exemplary damages, lost profits, lost revenue, business interruption, or lost data beyond the most recent backup. SGC Tech AI's sole backup obligation is daily backups with thirty-day retention; Client is responsible for any additional copies it requires.

13.3 Carve-outs, and their own cap. The cap in 13.1 does not apply to Client's payment obligations, to fraud, or to gross negligence or wilful misconduct. Liability for breach of confidentiality, breach of the intellectual property terms of Schedule D, or an indemnity under §14 is capped at three times the Subscription Fees paid in the preceding twelve months rather than being unlimited. COUNSEL

Why the carve-outs are themselves capped

An entirely uncapped confidentiality carve-out means a single data incident can exceed the total lifetime value of the engagement by orders of magnitude. For a company of SGC Tech AI's size that is an existential exposure rather than a commercial one. A multiple preserves the deterrent effect while keeping the exposure insurable. This clause and §17.3 must be read together — the cap is only credible if the insurance is actually in force.

13.4 AI Output. SGC Tech AI shall have no liability for loss arising from AI Output, including inaccurate, incomplete, fabricated or misleading output; biased or otherwise inappropriate output; Client's reliance on AI Output for a business, financial, legal, regulatory or strategic decision without independent human verification; or a third-party claim arising from Client's use or distribution of AI Output. Client acknowledges AI is assistive technology requiring human oversight.

13.5 Cybersecurity. Neither Party is liable for loss arising from a cybersecurity incident caused by a zero-day vulnerability in third-party software that was not publicly known or patchable at the time, provided the affected Party applied then-available patches in accordance with Schedule B.
