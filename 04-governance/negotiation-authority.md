# Negotiation Authority

What an SDR may say in a live negotiation without pausing to escalate,
versus what requires a Commercial Desk check first — a practical
companion to `approval-matrix.md` for the moment a client actually pushes
back on a number.

## An SDR may, without escalation

- State the list price and walk through §10 as drafted.
- Offer the standard 2 training sessions / adoption clause / clawback /
  price-lock terms as already included — these aren't negotiable extras,
  they're baseline.
- Explain the financing uplift and offer the "settle in full at kickoff
  removes it" framing (`clause-library/financing-disclosure.md`).
- Say no to a request that trips an abort criterion
  (`07-protection/abort/abort-criteria.md`) — and should say no, per G30,
  without treating it as a failure to escalate around.

## An SDR must escalate to Commercial Desk before agreeing to

- Any discount on the platform portion, any deferral of first payment,
  any free module/user, any term change, any go-live expediting — i.e.
  anything on `concession-ladder.yaml: concessions`. Reference the
  walk-away deal card's pre-selected top-3 compensators
  (`07-protection/walkaway/deal-card.template.md`) as the starting point
  for what to offer back, not an improvised trade.
- Any cadence other than the baseline (`payment-plans.yaml: baseline`)
  or the client's risk-appropriate minimum.
- Any request that would require a third pricing option or a
  zero-mobilisation structure — both are off the table entirely (see
  `runbook`: "two options, never three"; `payment-plans.yaml: withdrawn`).

## Never negotiable, regardless of who's asking

Everything in `concession-ladder.yaml: forbidden_compensators`, the
absolute floors in `approval-matrix.md`, and pricing exclusivity
(`clause-library/exclusivity-replacement.md`).

## Script for "can I get a better rate if I sign today"

> "I can offer full-term prepayment or annual-in-advance billing — both
> genuinely reduce the platform portion of your fee. What I can't do is
> discount the recovery portion, because that reflects work we've already
> committed hours to deliver, not a margin we're padding."
