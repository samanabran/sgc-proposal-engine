# Partnership Terms

## Term and commencement

The initial term commences on the date of kickoff, and the first
invoice — including mobilisation — is issued on that date. **24-month
initial term.** Following the initial term, this agreement renews
automatically for successive terms of equal length unless either party
provides written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the
then-current term ends, subject to Post-Recovery Continuation below.

## Adoption

The Client nominates a named data-entry owner responsible for day-to-day
system use. SGC TECH AI checks agreed activity thresholds at day 30
following go-live. If those thresholds are not met by day 60, SGC TECH AI
provides one remediation and retraining session at no additional charge.
Given that RVN's own biggest concern is agent adoption of anything
replacing the current WhatsApp/Sheets workflow, this is the specific,
dated, checkable commitment behind that concern — not a slogan.

## Clawback

If this subscription is terminated before the end of the committed term
for any reason other than SGC TECH AI's material breach, the unrecovered
balance of the implementation value becomes immediately due and payable.

## Price lock

The subscription fee quoted in this proposal is fixed for the full
24-month term and will not increase during that period, except where the
Client requests additional scope, priced separately as a change request.

## Security deposit

A refundable security deposit equal to one month's subscription fee
(AED 1,680) is required alongside mobilisation, reflecting this deal's
risk assessment. This is separate from, and does not reduce, the
mobilisation fee or any subscription payment.

## Post-recovery continuation

The implementation component is fully recovered by the end of the
24-month term. Thereafter the subscription continues month-to-month at
the same rate with 30 days' written notice. No reduction in the
subscription fee is implied.

## Data portability

The Client's data is exportable in full, on request, at any time —
including during any dispute or period of suspended service access. This
right is unconditional.
