# Executive Summary

RVN is a Dubai real-estate brokerage generating 400-600 new leads a month
from Meta and Google ad campaigns, currently managed entirely through a
shared Google Sheet and per-agent personal sheets. This proposal sets out
how SGC TECH AI will replace that manual workflow with a purpose-built
Odoo CRM — automated and one-click manual lead distribution, per-agent
call-outcome logging, and activity reporting against your team's daily
call target — built on Odoo Community.

## Scope snapshot

Lead capture and distribution from your existing Meta/Google ad pipeline
into the CRM; per-agent call logging (answered / not answered / follow-up
/ notes); a reporting dashboard tracking logged call activity against
your 250-calls/agent/day target; migration of your existing Google Sheet
lead records; and role-based access limited to your sales/telesales team
and brokers (7 users) — your marketing team and ownership are excluded
from CRM licensing, as requested. No billing/invoicing module, as
requested.

## Why now

Every lead your campaigns generate today passes through a manual
copy-and-shuffle step before an agent ever sees it, and once it does,
there is no reliable way to know whether it was called, when, or what
happened — the exact gap you described on our call. This proposal closes
that gap without adding a system your team has to fight to use.
