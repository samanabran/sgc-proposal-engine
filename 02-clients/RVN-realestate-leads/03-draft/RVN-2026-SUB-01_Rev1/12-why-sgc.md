Discovery-first, gate-checked pricing, and a fixed section structure for
every proposal we issue — this document itself is evidence of that
discipline: every commercial figure in §10 traces to an auditable
worksheet that passed 41 separate gate checks before reaching you.

## We told you what we don't have priced yet — on purpose

You raised call-analyzer/telephony integration and sensor-based
attendance tracking on our call. Rather than folding a guessed number
into this proposal to look complete, we've told you exactly what we need
from you to price those two items honestly (§07). That's a deliberate
choice, not a gap in our process.

## What sets us apart

- **Auditability by construction**: every number in this proposal traces
  to a source file, not a sales estimate.
- **Specialist-boutique pricing**: positioned below mid-tier agency
  rates without cutting the QA, documentation, or migration-validation
  work that keeps a go-live from breaking on day one.
- **We build for the workflow you actually have**: your agents work off
  personal SIM cards and WhatsApp today, not a company phone platform —
  our Phase 1 scope reflects that reality rather than assuming
  infrastructure you don't have yet.
