# Current State (As-Is)

## Lead intake and distribution

Leads from Meta and Google ad campaigns land in a shared Google Sheet.
Ms. Dia manually reviews the sheet and copies each lead into the
relevant agent's personal Google Sheet — a fully manual step repeated
for every one of the 400-600 leads arriving each month. Instant leads
also arrive via WhatsApp, sometimes actioned by direct call on WhatsApp
itself.

## Call and follow-up tracking

There is no system-level record of call activity. Agents self-report at
the end of the day, informally, in a group chat — a format, but not a
reliable one. RVN cannot currently see, for any given lead: whether it
has been called, how many times, whether the call was answered, or what
was discussed. A target of 250 calls/agent/day exists but is not
verifiable against any system record; the "call analyzer app" currently
in informal use is, in Ms. Dia's own words, "not so accurate."

## Attendance and breaks

RVN already has a physical sensor/badge system tracking entry and exit,
but the data isn't connected to any workforce system — staff previously
used a manual logbook for check-in/check-out, which RVN has since found
impractical.

## Prior tooling

Excel was used earlier and abandoned due to data-handling problems at
volume. Google Sheets replaced it for its relative simplicity but has
reached its own ceiling as lead volume has grown. RVN has evaluated 5-6
other CRM vendors (India and Dubai) and was not convinced by their
packages — no CRM has been adopted to date.
