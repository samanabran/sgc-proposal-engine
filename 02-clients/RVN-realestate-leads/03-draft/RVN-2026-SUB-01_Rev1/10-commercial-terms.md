# Commercial Terms — Two Options

Both options deliver identical Phase 1 scope (§06). They differ only in
how the build is paid for and the minimum term that follows from that.

## Why the terms differ

Option A carries no deferred balance — nothing is financed, so there is
nothing that requires an extended term to recover. Its 12-month minimum
covers onboarding and support cost-to-serve, not a repayment schedule.
Option B defers 67% of the build cost into the monthly fee; its 24-month
term is what allows that deferred amount to be repaid without raising the
monthly figure further.

**Crossover**: the two options cost effectively the same at roughly 20
months (AED 69 apart at that point, verified: month 20.14 exactly) — below
that, Option B is cheaper; beyond it, Option A is. This is a gradual
crossover, not a hard decision point at any single month.

## Payment cadence

Both options bill **quarterly in advance**.

## Financing disclosure

Option B's deferred remainder (AED 10,269) carries an 18% financing
component — AED 1,848 total, reflected as an AED 505/month addition to
the AED 1,170 platform fee. **This AED 505/month component is part of the
recurring subscription price, not a separately-tracked "recovery charge"**
— per the standard post-recovery continuation clause (§09), the monthly
fee does not step down once the deferred amount is fully repaid at month
24; it continues at AED 1,680/mo as an ordinary subscription price for the
remainder of the term and any renewal. Any reference to "recovery"
anywhere in this proposal describes how the fee was originally built, not
a temporary component that later disappears — see §09 for the governing
clause. Option A carries no financing component at all: the full build is
paid at kickoff, and the AED 1,170/mo fee is the platform cost-to-serve
only, unchanged for the life of the option.

## VAT — current position and a forward scenario

SGC TECH AI is not currently registered for UAE VAT, and no VAT is
charged on this proposal (`clause-library/vat-uae.md`). Because Option B
runs a 24-month term (and Option A can be renewed similarly), it is worth
stating plainly what happens if that changes: should SGC TECH AI become
VAT-registered during the term, VAT at the prevailing rate would be added
to invoices from that point forward, per `clause-library/vat-gross-up.md`
— **this is a tax-treatment question that should be confirmed with your
own accountant or counsel before signing a multi-year commitment**, not
something resolved definitively in this sales document.
