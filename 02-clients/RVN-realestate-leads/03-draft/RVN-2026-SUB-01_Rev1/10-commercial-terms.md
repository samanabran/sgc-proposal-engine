# Commercial Terms

## Pricing summary

| | Option A — mobilisation paid |
|---|---|
| Mobilisation (33% of build value) | AED 5,058 |
| Refundable security deposit (1 month) | AED 1,680 |
| Recurring subscription | AED 1,680/mo |
| Payable at Kickoff (mobilisation + deposit + Q1) | AED 11,778 |
| Year 1 total | AED 25,218 |
| Full 24-month commitment | AED 45,378 |

This is the only option offered — a zero-mobilisation structure is not
available at this time.

## Payment cadence

Subscription fees are billed **quarterly in advance** — AED 5,040 per
quarter (AED 1,680 × 3).

## Financing disclosure

Deferring the build carries an 18% financing component, reflected in the
AED 505/month recovery portion of your subscription fee. Settling the
build in full at kickoff removes this component entirely.
