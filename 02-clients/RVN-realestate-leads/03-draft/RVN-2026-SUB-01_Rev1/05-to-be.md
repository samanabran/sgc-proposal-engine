# Target State (To-Be)

## Phase 1 — this proposal

- Leads flow from your existing intake process into the CRM, distributed
  automatically where rules allow, with a one-click manual assignment
  option for Ms. Dia to override or direct specific leads.
- Every agent logs each call's outcome (answered / not answered /
  follow-up / notes) directly against the lead record — no more
  end-of-day recall or group-chat reporting.
- A reporting dashboard aggregates logged call activity per agent per
  day, giving management visibility against the 250-calls/day target
  without a manual daily report.
- Your existing Google Sheet lead history is migrated into the CRM so
  the switch doesn't start from zero.
- Role-based access limits CRM use to your sales/telesales team and
  brokers (7 seats) — marketing and ownership are not licensed users.

## Phase 2 — scoped separately, not priced in this proposal

- **Automated call-analyzer / telephony integration**: real, auto-
  captured call volume and pickup-rate metrics (rather than agent-logged
  activity) depend on integrating with whatever phone system you settle
  on — this proposal doesn't assume or price a specific one, since
  agents currently use personal SIM cards, not a company phone platform.
- **Attendance/break tracking via your existing sensor system**:
  integrating your current badge/sensor hardware into the CRM depends on
  that system's own vendor and API access, which we don't yet have
  visibility into.

Both Phase 2 items are real, logged requirements — not dropped. See §07
for how we propose to scope and price them once the missing information
is available.
