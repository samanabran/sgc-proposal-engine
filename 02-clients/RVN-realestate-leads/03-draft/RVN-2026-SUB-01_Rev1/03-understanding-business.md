# Understanding Your Business

RVN is a real-estate brokerage operating from Burjuman Business Towers,
Dubai, generating leads through Meta and Google ad campaigns at a scale
of roughly 400-600 new leads a month. Your sales operation runs seven
people — a telesales team and brokers — with lead assignment currently
performed manually by Ms. Dia via a shared Google Sheet and per-agent
personal sheets. Instant leads also arrive through WhatsApp.

Your marketing team and ownership are intentionally excluded from the
CRM you're evaluating — this system is scoped for the people who work
leads day to day, not the whole company.

## Decision-maker

Final approval for funds and commercial terms rests with Mr. Nazim, the
company's owner. Ms. Dia leads discovery and day-to-day lead assignment
and would be a CRM user herself.

## What you told us matters most

1. Getting leads out of the Google Sheet shuffle and into an assigned,
   trackable system — automatically where possible, with a one-click
   manual override.
2. Knowing what happened on every call — answered, unanswered, needs
   follow-up, with notes — instead of relying on end-of-day recall.
3. Visibility into whether your team is hitting its 250-calls/agent/day
   target, without you having to chase a daily group-chat report.
4. Attendance and break-time visibility, tied to the physical sensor
   system you already have in place.

No billing or invoicing functionality is wanted — that's explicitly out
of scope, not an oversight.
