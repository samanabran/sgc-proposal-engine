## Incumbent system replacement

This proposal assumes replacement of RVN's Google Sheet-based lead
tracking as the system of record for lead assignment and call activity
following the agreed go-live cutover. Where RVN intends to keep the
Google Sheet workflow running in parallel beyond an agreed validation
period, this constitutes a change to the assumptions underlying this
proposal's pricing and timeline, and must be raised as a change request
prior to acceptance.

## Recovery basis

This proposal's financing structure (§10, §09) assumes the

worksheet. This has been checked against G2 (Term ≥ recovery) in this
deal's gate report — recovery completes exactly at month 24.
