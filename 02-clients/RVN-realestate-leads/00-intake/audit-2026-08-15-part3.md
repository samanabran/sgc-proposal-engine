## A3 — Root-cause fix

Field names found: `financed_remainder_aed` on KP, PRO, VGE, RVN; `deferred_aed` on MRD only. Canonical name chosen: **`financed_remainder_aed`** (4 of 5 already use it, more descriptive). MRD's worksheet was **not** migrated in this pass — out of this pass's declared scope (RVN + repo-wide *check* audit, not a full MRD worksheet edit) and MRD's Rev3 worksheet wasn't opened; flagging this as incomplete rather than silently leaving the impression it's done. `check_13_clawback` still reads `financed_remainder_aed` first, `deferred_aed` second — the fallback stays **for MRD only**, which is a real, named exception (not blanket fallback logic preserving drift), documented inline in the code.

The deeper fix: `check_13_clawback` no longer treats a **genuinely absent** field as `0` (silently passing). If neither field name is present, it now cross-checks `build_value_aed` vs mobilisation; if that implies a real deferred remainder, it fails with an explicit "cross-check surfaced an undisclosed deferred structure" message rather than assuming zero. If even the cross-check data is missing, it fails closed ("cannot verify").

`check_9_10_cadence_mobilisation` had the same class of bug, worse in one respect: it printed a **fabricated** "OK" (`cadence 'None' meets or exceeds minimum`) when `payment_cadence` was entirely unset — not just silent, actively misleading. Fixed to fail closed. This surfaced that **no worksheet in the repo except MRD ever recorded `payment_cadence`** at all, despite every draft describing quarterly billing in prose. Added `payment_cadence: quarterly_in_advance` to KP, PRO, and RVN worksheets (matching what their drafts already state). **VGE was deliberately left unset** — its worksheet/gate-report claim "quarterly_in_advance" (G12, G33 both marked PASS) but its Rev3 draft (`10-commercial-terms.md:19`) says **"billed monthly in advance"**. That is a real, unresolved contradiction between what was gated and what's in the client-facing draft — not mine to silently resolve by picking one. Flagged for whoever owns VGE.

## C — G23 cushion, verified and reframed

**Reframed headline**: *Passes only at an unvalidated cost basis that is not plausible for a UAE FZCO with visas, medical cover, gratuity and leave. Option B breaches on a 14% cost increase alone.* The passing case assumes salary is the entire cost of employment and staff bill 96% of paid hours — neither holds for this entity type. No real burden multiplier is invented here; the point is only that the current one (1.0×) is not a credible assumption to sign a 24–36 month commitment against.

**Consequence**: these options may be underpriced, and that question should close before a 24–36 month client commitment is signed, not after.
