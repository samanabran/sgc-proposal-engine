## Item 2 — G23 sensitivity table (all cells PRV — illustrative stress
test on existing cost figures, not sourced from any real payroll/burden
table, because no such table exists in this repo — confirmed by grepping
`policy.yaml` and `rate-card.yaml` for "burden"/"utilisation": zero
matches, no such field exists anywhere)

Baseline (1.0x burden, 96% utilisation) reproduces the worksheet's own
figures exactly: Option A 12mo margin 36.72%, Option B 24mo margin
34.30%. Utilisation factor = 0.96 / U (cost scales up as utilisation
drops below the 96% baseline).

| Burden \ Utilisation | 96% (baseline) | 80% | 65% |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1.0x** | A 36.7% PASS · B 34.3% PASS | A 24.1% BREACH · B 21.2% BREACH | A 6.6% BREACH · B 3.0% BREACH |
| **1.2x** | A 24.1% BREACH · B 21.2% BREACH | A 8.9% BREACH · B 5.4% BREACH | A −12.2% BREACH · B −16.3% BREACH |
| **1.4x** | A 11.4% BREACH · B 8.0% BREACH | A −6.3% BREACH · B −10.4% BREACH | A −30.8% BREACH · B −35.9% BREACH |

Combined multiplier = burden × (0.96/utilisation); note 1.0x/80% and
1.2x/96% land on the identical combined multiplier (1.2x) and therefore
identical margins — not a duplicate-row error.

Breach thresholds (exact): Option A crosses below 25% at combined
multiplier > **1.185x**; Option B — more fragile — crosses at combined
multiplier > **1.142x**. Both options are still comfortably PASS at the
current unvalidated baseline, but the margin is thin: a burden or
utilisation swing well inside plausible real-world range (e.g. 80%
utilisation alone, no burden increase at all) already breaches both.

**Rewritten G23 line**: "No breaches at the current unvalidated cost
basis (1.0x burden, 96% utilisation). At combined burden×utilisation
multiplier ≥1.19x (e.g. 1.0x burden / 80% utilisation, or 1.2x burden /
96% utilisation), Option A breaches. Option B breaches slightly earlier,
at ≥1.14x. Every multiplier in this table is PRV — no real payroll or

## Item 5 — Gate discrepancy: real validator bug, found and fixed

**Root cause**: `05-ops/validate.py` `check_13_clawback` reads
`ws["number_3_financing"]["deferred_aed"]` — but every worksheet in this
repo except MRD's uses the field name `financed_remainder_aed`
(confirmed by grep across all five clients: KP/PRO/VGE/RVN use
`financed_remainder_aed`; only MRD kept the old `deferred_aed`). The
check was silently reading a missing key as `0` on four of five clients,
always taking the "no deferred value" branch regardless of the real
financed remainder. **Not caused by the new `option_b_milestone_full_upfront`
block** — that block correctly has no deferred remainder (it's a
same-day-payment structure); the bug predates it and is repo-wide.

**Fix applied** (`05-ops/validate.py`, `check_13_clawback`): now reads
`financed_remainder_aed` first, falls back to `deferred_aed` for MRD
compatibility. Low-risk, three-line change. Re-ran `validate.py` against
all five clients post-fix:
