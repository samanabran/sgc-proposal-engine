04 — Proposed solution

This proposal is an Odoo subscription. SGC Tech AI builds it and supports it, start to finish. Your version is pinned: the version we build stays the version you run, until you choose to upgrade.

Upgrade policy

Odoo does not push automatic upgrades. No vendor does — this is normal, not a limit specific to us.

Our rule is simple: stay on your version until you choose to upgrade. When you do, we treat it as its own project, at a fixed price. We never roll upgrade costs into your monthly fee.

Here is how an upgrade works. First, we copy your live data to a test copy. Then we run the upgrade there, using OCA's proven upgrade tools where we can, or Odoo's official upgrade path where that fits better. We check the data and test everything. Only then do we move your live system over.

Result: your system stays stable between upgrades. You choose when to upgrade, and you know the cost before you say yes.

What you get in plain language

  • Listings and units. Every property you manage, with status, project, location, and availability — visible to your whole team.
  • Leads and deals. Every lead lands in one place, from any source. Once Phase 2 is live, your website leads land here too. Follow each lead through to a closed deal. See which agent is performing.
  • Tenancies, contracts, invoices. Track rental income and contract terms. Get an automatic reminder before a contract expires — so you never miss a renewal.
  • Maintenance to invoice. Log a repair request, price it, and turn it into an invoice — without typing it twice.
  • Reports and dashboards. See your revenue, your properties, and your agents' activity at a glance — not buried in a spreadsheet.
  • Customer and vendor records. Store your customers' and suppliers' tax details in the system, where they apply.

You and your agents can use this on your phone's browser. There is no separate app to install — see §07 for what is not included.

05 — How the subscription works

This is the answer to what you told us about budget. In plain words:

  • Your full build is worth AED 14,800. We tell you this number up front. You will never see it as a separate invoice.
  • At Kickoff, you pay AED 4,900. That is 33% of the build value. It covers the cost of starting the work, and it is non-refundable.
  • We finance the remaining AED 9,900 for you. You pay it back through your monthly fee, over 24 months.
  • Your monthly fee is AED 1,650. Two parts make up this fee: AED 1,163 for the platform (hosting, backup, monitoring, patching, support, account management), and AED 487 to repay the financed build, plus a disclosed 18% financing cost.
  • After 24 months, the build is fully paid off. Your fee stays the same and continues month-to-month. Either side can end it with 30 days' written notice — see MSA & SLA §A.14. The fee does not drop automatically once the build is paid.
  • You pay AED 4,950 every quarter, in advance.

The 18% is the cost of us carrying that AED 9,900 for you, for 24 months. Want to skip it? Pay the AED 9,900 build value in full at Kickoff instead, and your monthly fee drops to just the platform part.

What the constraint bought you

On the call, you asked about AED 30,000 and AED 40,000 as upfront, one-time build costs. Either number would have used up cash you told us you want to keep free. This structure fixes that. Instead of a big cheque, you pay AED 9,850 at Kickoff — far less than the AED 30,000–40,000 you were quoted elsewhere. The table below shows the full comparison.

09 — Adoption commitment

This section exists because of what you told us: your last systems stopped being used within two months. We built this section to fix that. It works both ways.

  • Within two weeks of Go-Live, you name one person responsible for using the system day to day.
  • At day 30, we check real usage — deals, leads, contracts, invoices — against a target we agree together at Kickoff.
  • If usage falls short by day 60, we run one free retraining session to fix it. No extra charge.
  • Keep that named person in the role during the Initial Term. If they leave, give us two weeks' notice so we can prepare the next person.

This protects both of us from a rollout that stalls. We commit to actively helping your team use the system. You commit to having one person accountable for it.
