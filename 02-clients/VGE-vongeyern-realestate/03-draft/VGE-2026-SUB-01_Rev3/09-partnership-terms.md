# Partnership Terms

## Term and commencement

The initial term of this agreement is **24 months**, commencing on the
date of the first invoice issued following signature of this proposal
("Commencement Date"), and not on the signature date itself where the two
differ. Where implementation mobilisation precedes the Commencement Date,
work performed during mobilisation is governed by this agreement's terms
from the date such work begins, notwithstanding that formal Commencement
has not yet occurred.

Following the initial term, this agreement renews automatically for
successive terms of equal length unless either party provides written
notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to the end of the
then-current term, subject to Post-Recovery Continuation below.

## Adoption

SGC TECH AI's implementation fee includes **2 training session(s)** of
**2 hour(s)** each, delivered during or immediately following go-live,
intended to establish baseline user competency across the Client's team.
Adoption of the delivered solution by the Client's end users beyond this
baseline training is a shared responsibility: SGC TECH AI will deliver the
training and documentation described in this proposal, and the Client is
responsible for internal change management, user onboarding cadence, and
ongoing process compliance following go-live.

Additional training beyond the sessions included in this proposal, or
recurring adoption-support engagements, may be scoped and quoted
separately and are not included by default in the fees quoted here.

## Clawback

Where any portion of the implementation fee has been deferred, discounted,
or waived by SGC TECH AI conditional upon the Client's continued
subscription for the initial term ("Deferred Value"), and the Client
terminates this agreement prior to the end of the initial term for any
reason other than SGC TECH AI's uncured material breach, the Client shall
pay to SGC TECH AI the unrecovered portion of the Deferred Value,
calculated on a straight-line basis across the initial term as of the
effective date of termination.

For the avoidance of doubt, this clause applies to the Deferred Value only
and does not constitute a penalty for early termination beyond recovering
value already delivered but not yet paid for.

## Post-recovery continuation

Once the implementation investment referenced in this agreement's
financing structure has been fully recovered through the initial term (as
established at the time of this proposal per the applicable pricing
worksheet — recovery completes at month 24), the recurring subscription
fee for any renewal term will be recalculated to remove the financing
uplift described in §10 Commercial Terms, reflecting the ongoing cost of
licensing, hosting, and support alone. SGC TECH AI will notify the Client
in writing of the adjusted fee no less than thirty (30) days before the
renewal term begins.

This adjustment applies only to the financing uplift component. Standard
annual price adjustments for licensing, hosting, and support, if any, are
governed separately and communicated on the same notice schedule.
