# Support & SLA

## Support tier

| Tier | Response SLA | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Priority | 4-hour response | AED 1,650/mo — `support-training.yaml: support.priority_4hr_sla` |

## Training included

2 sessions of 2 hours each, included in the implementation fee (billed
once — Commercial Rule 8). Sessions are scheduled around Yusuf's
availability as primary system administrator, with the wider agent team
joining the second session. Additional training available at
`support-training.yaml: training.*` rates (e.g. Admin Training — Advanced,
AED 2,750/session, if deeper administrator training is wanted later).

## What's covered

Bug fixes, configuration questions, and minor adjustments within the
delivered scope, with a 4-hour response SLA. Trust/commission accounting
configuration questions are included under this tier given the criticality
flagged in discovery. New module requests, custom development, or scope
beyond §06/§07 are change requests, priced separately at current rate-card
rates.
