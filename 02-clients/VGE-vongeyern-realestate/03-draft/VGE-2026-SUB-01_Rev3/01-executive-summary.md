# Executive Summary

Von Geyern Real Estate Brokerage LLC is a Dubai mainland brokerage
currently running deal tracking on spreadsheets and commission/trust
accounting on a legacy desktop tool, with no CRM. This proposal sets out
how SGC TECH AI will bring your leads-to-close pipeline, trust and
commission accounting, and client service into a single Odoo platform —
so a deal's financial trail is never a step behind the deal itself.

## Scope snapshot

CRM, Sales, and Accounting for 12 users today, scaling to 18 within 12
months, including full trust/commission accounting configuration —
identified in discovery as your highest-risk gap. Migration of
approximately 850 historical deal records and 400 contract/title
documents is included.
