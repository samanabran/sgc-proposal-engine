# Target State

## The outcome

Every deal, from lead to closed commission, lives in one system. Trust and
commission accounting is configured to follow deal stage automatically,
with an audit trail that holds up to RERA/DLD scrutiny without manual
reconciliation. Management gets real pipeline visibility for the first
time.

## Phased approach

**Phase 1** (this proposal): CRM, Sales, and Accounting (including
trust/commission configuration), migration of historical deals and
documents, and standard UAE VAT localization.

**Phase 2** (deferred, priced separately): AI Lead Scorer (Lite tier) to
help prioritize inbound leads, and an AI Chatbot (Lite tier) for
first-response client queries — both discussed as future interest, not
included or priced in this proposal. See §07.

## What changes for your team

- **Agents**: log and track deals in one CRM pipeline instead of
  spreadsheets; visibility into their own commission status in real time.
- **Yusuf (Office Manager)**: runs day-to-day system administration;
  receives dedicated hands-on training (see §11) ahead of go-live.
- **Elena (Managing Partner)**: pipeline and financial reporting
  available without waiting on manual reconciliation.
