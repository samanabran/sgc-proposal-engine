# Current State

## Systems in use today

Deal and contact tracking: spreadsheets, maintained manually by agents,
with no shared pipeline visibility. Accounting: a legacy desktop tool
handling general ledger and commission calculation, not integrated with
the deal pipeline. No CRM.

## Process pain points

- Deal stage and commission status live in two disconnected systems —
  reconciling them is a manual, recurring task.
- No shared visibility into pipeline for management or across agents.
- Trust account handling depends on manual discipline rather than
  system-enforced controls.

## Constraints and dependencies

- RERA/DLD licensing and portal (e.g. listing feed) access remain the
  Client's responsibility — SGC's integration scope depends on the
  Client's existing verified access; see §07 assumptions.
- Data quality on the 850 historical deal records is rated medium
  confidence at intake — migration scope includes a validation pass,
  not a guarantee of full historical accuracy.
