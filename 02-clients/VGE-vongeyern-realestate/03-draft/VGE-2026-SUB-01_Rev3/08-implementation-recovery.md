## Incumbent system replacement

This proposal assumes replacement of the Client's spreadsheet-based deal
tracking and legacy desktop accounting tool. SGC TECH AI's scope and
pricing are based on this replacement being exclusive — the delivered
Odoo environment becomes the Client's system of record for CRM, Sales,
and Accounting following the agreed go-live cutover, and the legacy
desktop accounting tool is decommissioned or restricted to read-only
archive access at that point.

Where the Client intends to run the legacy accounting tool in parallel
beyond an agreed validation period, this constitutes a change to the
assumptions underlying this proposal's pricing and timeline, and must be
raised as a change request prior to acceptance.

## Recovery basis

This proposal's financing structure (§10, §09) assumes the implementation
investment is recovered over 24 months per the pricing worksheet. This has
been checked against G2 (Term ≥ recovery) in this deal's gate report —
recovery completes exactly at month 24.
