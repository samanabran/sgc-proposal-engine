# Understanding Your Business

## Who you are

Von Geyern Real Estate Brokerage LLC, a Dubai mainland brokerage, with 12
users today and an expected 18 within 12 months. Decision maker: Elena von
Geyern, Managing Partner.

## What you told us

Deal tracking currently lives in spreadsheets, with no CRM in place.
Commission and trust accounting runs on a legacy desktop tool disconnected
from the deal pipeline — the concern you raised most strongly in
discovery was getting this connection right, since a trust-accounting
error is a regulatory problem, not just an operational inconvenience. You
independently gathered a competing quote and separately received (and
declined) a considerably higher Big-4-style quote before approaching us.

## What matters most to you

1. Trust/commission accounting done correctly and auditably — the single
   highest-priority item from discovery.
2. A pipeline view that reflects actual deal stage, not a static
   spreadsheet.
3. Predictable monthly cash flow — the reason behind extending this
   proposal's term to 24 months.
