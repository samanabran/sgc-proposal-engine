Executive Summary

Prosper Intl Real Estate runs a 31-person team on spreadsheets and

WhatsApp-based client communication, with manual attendance tracking and

no unified CRM or ERP. The core gap is not one missing feature — it is the

absence of any single system of record: leads, client history, listings,

and attendance are each tracked differently, or not tracked at all. This

proposal sets out a Subscription Recovery Model to bring lead management,

listings, invoicing, and reporting into one Odoo platform, with no

separate implementation invoice.

Scope snapshot

Property/unit register, tenancy and contract tracking, TRN-compliant

invoicing, centralized CRM with customizable lead fields, agent role/

permission setup for multiple admins, reporting dashboards, and migration

of 500 historical records — sized for your full 31-person team.

One thing this proposal does not cover, flagged directly rather than

left implicit: attendance/check-in tracking and payroll/salary/WPS

processing have no priceable basis in SGC TECH AI's current delivery

catalogue for this vertical (see §07). Mobile access is delivered via a

fully responsive browser experience rather than an installable app — see

§07.

Relevant to real estate brokerage

A brokerage running on spreadsheets and WhatsApp does not fail because any

single tool is bad — it fails because nothing connects: a lead captured in

one place, a listing tracked in another, and client history that lives in

whoever's phone last spoke to the client. Our base scope for this

engagement (property register, CRM, invoicing, and reporting as one

connected system) is built to close that specific gap, not to bolt a

generic CRM on top of the same fragmentation.
