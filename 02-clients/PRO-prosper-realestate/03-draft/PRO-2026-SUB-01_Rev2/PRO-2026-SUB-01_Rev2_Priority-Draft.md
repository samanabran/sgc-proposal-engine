## 1. Executive Summary

Prosper's leads arrive from more than one place — property portals, direct
enquiries, referrals — and today nothing forces a paid lead through a
controlled pipeline before it can be marked lost or simply go quiet. Every
lead that scatters or falls through is money already spent. This is the
priority this draft leads with.

## 2. Priority Requirement — Unified Lead Capture

**The problem, as raised:** leads come in from multiple sources — portal
listings, direct enquiries, referrals — and get scattered across agents and
channels with no single system of record. Nothing currently stops a lead
from being pushed straight to "Not Interested" or "Lost" without a reason
logged, so leads that cost real money to acquire go missing quietly, and
management only finds out after the fact.

**What's included (base scope — already priced, already gate-passed):**

- **One centralized CRM** for every lead regardless of source — portal, direct, referral, or walk-in — so nothing lives only in an agent's phone or a spreadsheet.
- **Stage-gated pipeline**: a lead cannot be moved to a closed/lost stage without a reason logged first. No more silent drops.
- **Role-based visibility**: admins and management see the full pipeline and stage-movement history; agents see what's relevant to them.

**Priority add-on (optional, Phase 2 — quoted separately, never bundled into base scope):**

**Portal Sync** — direct automated feed from Property Finder and Bayut
straight into the same CRM, so a portal lead lands in the system the
moment it arrives instead of being manually re-keyed (or missed).

| Item | Price | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Property Finder sync | AED 3,900 | one-time |
| Bayut / Dubizzle sync | AED 3,400 | one-time |

This is conditional on the client side, not SGC: a valid RERA/DLD licence
and agency ID, the agency's own portal-side verification being complete,
listings meeting the portal's image/data standards, and an active portal
subscription that includes feed/API access. SGC's fee covers building the
sync — the client's own portal subscription remains a separate, ongoing
cost that SGC does not quote.

## 4. Commercial Structure

### 4a. Build Cost — Paid Off Monthly (no separate invoice)

Rather than a large one-time implementation fee, the build is recovered
**inside** the subscription across the term.

| | AED |
|---|---|
| Total implementation value | 55,006 |
| Mobilisation at Kickoff (40% — elevated risk band) | 22,002 |
| Remaining build value, paid off monthly | 33,004 |

| Term | Monthly build-payoff instalment | Instalments |
|---|---|---|
| **24 months (preferred)** | **AED 1,623/mo** | 24 |
| 12 months (alternative) | AED 2,915/mo | 12 |

> Deferring the build carries a **18%** financing component (24-month
> option; **6%** on the 12-month option), reflected in the recovery
> portion of your subscription fee. Settling the build in full at kickoff
> removes this component entirely.

> If this subscription is terminated before the end of the committed term
> for any reason other than SGC TECH AI's material breach, the unrecovered
> balance of the implementation value becomes immediately due and payable.
