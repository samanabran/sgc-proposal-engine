# Support & SLA

## Support tier

| Tier | Response SLA | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Priority | 4-hour response | AED 1,650/mo — `support-training.yaml: support.priority_4hr_sla` |

Priority tier reflects the elevated risk band on this deal — a faster
response SLA is a reasonable pairing with the additional security
instruments in §09, not an upsell.

## Training included

2 sessions of 2 hours each, included in the implementation fee (billed
once). Sessions are scheduled around Dian Sajulga's availability as
primary point of contact, with the wider team joining across the sessions
given the 31-person headcount. Additional training available at
`support-training.yaml: training.*` rates (e.g. Admin Training —
Advanced, AED 2,750/session).

## What's covered

Bug fixes, configuration questions, and minor adjustments within the
delivered scope, with a 4-hour response SLA. New module requests, custom
development, or scope beyond §06/§07 are change requests, priced
separately at current rate-card rates — including, notably, any future
attendance/payroll/AI-assistant scoping, which is not a "minor
adjustment" but new catalogue work.

## Service credit guarantees

| Guarantee | Credit | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Go-live timeliness | 5% of one month's subscription per week late | 2 months' subscription |
| Adoption | One free remediation/retraining session | 1 per term (see §09) |
| UAT re-performance | Re-performed at no charge | Never a refund |
| Price lock | Rate fixed for the term | — |
| Uptime (SGC-managed hosting only) | 1 month's subscription credit below 99.5% uptime | 1 month per year |
| Data portability | Unconditional export | — |
| Exit | No exit fee; unrecovered clawback balance only | — |

**Aggregate credit cap: 10% of annual contract value across all
guarantees combined, per term.**

> These guarantees are void where the triggering delay or shortfall is
> caused by the Client — including late provision of data files, delayed
> portal feed access, or unavailability of the Client's nominated system
> owner — and are void during any period of non-payment.
