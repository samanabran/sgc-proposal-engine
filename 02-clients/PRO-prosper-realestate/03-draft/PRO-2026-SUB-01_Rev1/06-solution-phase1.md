## What "done" looks like for this phase

- Every lead visible in a centralized pipeline with customizable fields,
  no additional-user surcharge for the team as currently sized.
- A unified property/unit register replacing scattered listing tracking.
- Role-based access separating agent, admin, and management visibility —
  the "multiple admins" requirement directly addressed.
- TRN-compliant invoicing tied to the underlying deal/tenancy record.
- Full 31-person team onboarded with role-appropriate access.
- Live reporting replacing manual compilation.

## Migration scope

500 historical records from up to two source files. Data quality
confidence is unknown at this stage — not yet assessed against source
data, since this deal has not reopened with the client.

## What this phase does not include

Attendance/check-in tracking, payroll/salary structure/WPS processing, and
a ChatGPT/Copilot-style AI assistant are **not** part of this phase and
have no priceable basis anywhere in SGC TECH AI's current delivery
catalogue for this vertical — see §07 for the full treatment of each.
