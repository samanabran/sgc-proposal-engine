A generic CRM requires extensive customization to match UAE real estate
workflows and still typically leaves listings and finance as separate
systems. Building in-house carries a multi-month timeline and ongoing
engineering cost with no equivalent recovery structure. SGC TECH AI
delivers a domain-specific, connected platform deployed in weeks rather
than months — for the scope this proposal actually prices; see §07 for
what remains genuinely outside that scope today.
