# Commercial Terms

## Pricing summary

| | 24-month term (preferred) | 12-month term |
|---|---|---|
| Mobilisation (at Kickoff) | AED 38,544 | AED 38,544 |
| Recurring subscription | AED 6,490/mo | AED 8,760/mo |
| Billed quarterly in advance | AED 19,470 | AED 26,280 |
| Year 1 total | AED 116,424 | n/a — full term is 12mo |
| Full term commitment | AED 194,304 | AED 143,664 |

**24-month term is recommended** — genuinely lower monthly cost, purely
from spreading the same recovery principal over a longer period (no
discount applied to the recovery component itself). Both options carry
the same mobilisation fee, independent of term choice.

**Monthly billing** is available as an alternative to the standard
quarterly-in-advance cadence, at a **+3% surcharge** (AED 6,680/mo on the
24-month term) — offered specifically to match cash-flow patterns the
Client may already be used to, at the cost of a modest premium for the
more frequent billing cycle.

## Payment schedule

Mobilisation (AED 38,544) due at Kickoff, per standard MSA v2026.08 §5.2
terms — non-refundable except on Client termination-for-cause. Security
deposit and post-dated cheques per §09 due prior to Kickoff. Recurring
subscription billed quarterly in advance from the Commencement Date (or
monthly, at the surcharge above, if elected).

## Financing disclosure

> Deferring the build carries an 18% financing component, reflected in
> the recovery portion of your subscription fee (24-month term). Settling
> the build in full at kickoff removes this component entirely.

The 12-month term carries a 6% financing component instead, per
`pricing/policy.yaml: financing_uplift`. This uplift does not vary by
negotiation — only by the term length selected.
