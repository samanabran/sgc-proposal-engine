# Answers to Your CRM & ERP Questions

**Prepared for:** Dian Sajulga, Prosper Intl Real Estate
**Re:** Your 14 questions, CRM & ERP Requirements document (21 July 2026)

Every answer below is either a direct yes with the specific capability
named, a partial answer with the boundary stated plainly, or a direct no
— nothing softened. Nine items from your Must Have / Nice to Have lists
have no answer we can give in the affirmative today; they're listed at
the end rather than folded quietly into "yes, with customization."

---

**1. Can your system support CRM, admin, KPI tracking, and ERP features in one platform?**

**Partial.** Yes for CRM (leads, clients, deal pipeline), admin/role
management, and KPI/reporting dashboards — all in one Odoo platform.
**Not included in the current quote**: TRN-compliant invoicing (a real
capability, just not in this scope — see the gap list below), task
management, calendar/scheduling, approval workflows, or commission
tracking as standalone modules.

**2. Can we customize the fields based on our company requirements?**

**Yes.** Field-level customization is a core platform capability.

**3. Does the system have built-in KPI dashboards?**

**Yes.** Reporting dashboards are in current scope.

**4. Can we track meetings, referrals, listings, and deals per agent?**

**Partial.** Listings and deals per agent: yes, core to current scope.
Meetings and referrals: the CRM includes standard activity/interaction
logging (calls and meetings recorded against each client record), but a
dedicated "referral" field or a named referral-tracking report is a
small configuration item, not separately scoped or priced today.

**5. Can we manage attendance with Present, Late, and Absent statuses?**

**No — not today.** No attendance/HR module exists in our current
delivery catalogue for this vertical. This would need a real scoping
exercise before it could be quoted at all.

**6. Can Late and Absent attendance require a reason?**

**No — not today.** Depends on the attendance module in Q5, which is
not in current scope.

**7. Can we upload and organize documents and property files?**

**Partial.** Property-related documents (photos, title deeds, NOCs,
floor plans) are included with each listing, core to current scope.
General document management across contracts, IDs, agreements, and KYC
forms beyond property files is not separately scoped today.

**8. Can we assign listings and leads to specific agents?**

**Yes.** Core to current scope.

**9. Can the system be accessed through mobile and desktop?**

**Yes, with one boundary stated plainly.** Full desktop access, and full
mobile access through a responsive mobile browser — not a downloadable
app. Every capability below works the same on a phone as on a desktop;
there's just nothing to install.

**10. Can we export reports to Excel?**

**Yes.** Standard capability across all reporting.

**11. What is the monthly cost per user?**

**AED 4,560 per month, flat, covering a band of up to 35 users** — not a
per-seat charge, so it doesn't change within that range. Every role your
own document lists as needing system access (Agent, Admin, Manager, CEO)
counts toward the band. Worked out per person at your current headcount
of 31 (itself **not yet confirmed** — see the question below), that's
**AED 147/user/month, shown here only as an illustration of what the
flat fee works out to, not a separate quoted rate.** If you grow past 35,
we re-quote for the next band rather than scaling automatically.

**12. Is there a setup fee or customization fee?**

**AED 12,366 at kickoff** — covering the scope that maps directly to
your own Must Have list (see the gap list below for what this figure
does *not* include), not our full standard package.

**13. Do you provide training for our team?**

**Yes.** Included in current scope.

**14. Do you provide support after setup?**

**Yes.** Ongoing support is built into the standard monthly service, not
a separate add-on.
