# Target State

## The outcome

PropSpace and the spreadsheet workaround are both retired. Property,
tenancy, invoicing, and maintenance-to-invoice all live in one system —
and because adoption is engineered in from day 30, this is the change
that sticks where the last two didn't.

## Phased approach

**Phase 1** (this proposal): property/tenancy register, renewal
reminders, TRN-linked invoicing, maintenance-to-invoice, CRM and agent
performance visibility, mobile-optimised browser access.

**Phase 2** (deferred, priced separately): portal sync to Property
Finder, Bayut, and Dubizzle; website lead capture. Not included in this
proposal's pricing — see §07.

## What changes for your team

- **Agents**: log deals and see performance visibility in one place
  instead of raw activity counts.
- **Office administrator (nominated data-entry owner)**: runs day-to-day
  system use; day-30 usage checkpoint against agreed activity thresholds
  (§09).
- **Omar**: tenancy renewal reminders that actually fire, and invoicing
  that doesn't require re-typing a maintenance request.
