# Support & SLA

## Support tier

|---|---|---|

## Service credit guarantees

| Guarantee | Credit | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Go-live timeliness | 5% of one month's subscription per week late | 2 months' subscription |
| Adoption | One free remediation/retraining session | 1 per term |
| UAT re-performance | Re-performed at no charge | Never a refund |
| Price lock | Rate fixed for the term | — |
| Data portability | Unconditional export | — |
| Exit | No exit fee; unrecovered clawback balance only | — |

**Aggregate credit cap: 10% of annual contract value (AED 1,680 × 12 ×
10% = AED 2,016) across all guarantees combined, per term.**

These guarantees are void where the triggering delay or shortfall is
caused by the Client — including late provision of data files or
unavailability of the nominated data-entry owner — and are void during
any period of non-payment.

## Training included

2 sessions of 2 hours each, included in the implementation fee.

## What's covered

Configuration questions and minor adjustments within delivered scope.
Additional user, additional modules, or Phase 2 items are quoted
separately per §07.
