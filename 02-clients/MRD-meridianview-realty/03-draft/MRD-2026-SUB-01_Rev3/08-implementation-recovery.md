# Implementation & Recovery

## Timeline

| Milestone | Target | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Kickoff (term and first invoice both start here) | Week 1 | Plan, RAID log |
| Discovery and data validation complete | Week 2 | Validated migration scope |
| Configuration and migration | Weeks 3–5 | Property/tenancy register, invoicing, CRM live |
| Training | Week 6 | Two sessions delivered |
| Go-live | Week 6 | §06 "what done looks like" criteria met |

## Incumbent system replacement

This proposal assumes replacement of PropSpace and the spreadsheet-based
invoicing workaround. The delivered Odoo environment becomes your system
of record for property, tenancy, invoicing, and CRM following the agreed
go-live cutover.

Where the Client intends to run PropSpace in parallel beyond an agreed
validation period, this constitutes a change to the assumptions
underlying this proposal's pricing and timeline, and must be raised as a
change request prior to acceptance.

## Recovery basis

This proposal's financing structure assumes the implementation
investment is recovered over 24 months, completing exactly at term end.
Checked against G2 in `02-calc/gate-report.md`.
