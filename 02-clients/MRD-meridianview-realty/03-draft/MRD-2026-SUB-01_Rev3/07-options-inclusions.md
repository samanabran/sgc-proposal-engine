Each portal sync depends on: a valid RERA/DLD licence and agency RERA ID,

feed/API access — the portal subscription fee itself remains your own

## Assumptions

- Migration data quality is medium confidence; the discovery work package
  includes a validation pass, not a guarantee of full historical
  accuracy.
- RERA/DLD licensing status and portal feed access, if Phase 2 is taken
  up later, remain the Client's responsibility.
- The nominated data-entry owner is available for the discovery and
  requirements sessions within the proposed 6-week timeline.

## Exclusions

The following are excluded from the scope and pricing of this proposal
unless explicitly listed as an included module, work package, or option
elsewhere in this document: third-party software licensing costs not
explicitly listed in this proposal's commercial terms; custom
development or configuration beyond the listed work packages; data
migration from source systems not identified in the Client's brief, or
migration volumes materially exceeding the figures stated there; ongoing
change management or user adoption activity beyond the training sessions
included in this proposal; hardware procurement, on-premise
infrastructure, or network setup at the Client's premises; regulatory,
tax, or legal advisory services; integration with third-party systems
not explicitly named in this proposal's scope; support and maintenance
beyond the tier and term specified; and travel and accommodation beyond
what is explicitly itemized. Any work requested outside this list is
treated as a change request and quoted separately, at the rates current
in SGC TECH AI's rate card at the time of the request.
