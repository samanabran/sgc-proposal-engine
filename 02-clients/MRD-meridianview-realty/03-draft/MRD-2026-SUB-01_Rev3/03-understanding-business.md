# Understanding Your Business

## Who you are

Meridianview Realty LLC, a Dubai mainland brokerage, 5 users today,
expected 6 within 12 months. Decision maker: Omar Al Farsi, Owner /
Principal Broker.

## What you told us

You currently run PropSpace for listings and CRM, with invoicing handled
separately on spreadsheets. You received a prior quote north of AED
30,000 and it felt disproportionate to a 5-person operation. You
previously received two other proposals from us that didn't meet our own
standards — see §12 for how this one is different, concretely, not just
in tone.

## What matters most to you

1. **Adoption.** Stated directly on our discovery call as the thing that
   has killed your last two system changes — not a secondary concern.
2. Property, tenancy, and renewal tracking that actually replaces
   PropSpace rather than running alongside it.
3. Visibility into which agent is actually closing deals.
4. Fair pricing proportionate to a 5-person operation, not a scaled-down
   version of an enterprise quote.
