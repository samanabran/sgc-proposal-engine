# Solution — Phase 1

## Edition

Your platform is built on Odoo, the world's most widely deployed
open-source business platform, running on infrastructure managed
entirely by SGC TECH AI. There are no per-user licence fees — which is
precisely why we can absorb the implementation into your subscription
rather than invoicing it upfront.

**This proposal is built on Odoo Community edition.** Community includes
CRM, sales, invoicing, basic accounting, project, maintenance, contacts,
website, and mobile-optimised browser access — **not** a dedicated
iOS/Android app. It does not include automated bank reconciliation,
Studio (no-code customization), advanced tax configuration,
multi-currency, or Odoo's own vendor upgrade service and official
support — SGC TECH AI provides implementation and support directly
instead.

**Upgrade policy**: version-pinned, estimated at 12 hours per major
version if and when you choose to upgrade.

None of your stated requirements trigger a need for Enterprise edition —
if that changes (e.g. you need automated bank reconciliation or a native
mobile app), we'll say so explicitly and price the upgrade separately,
never silently.
