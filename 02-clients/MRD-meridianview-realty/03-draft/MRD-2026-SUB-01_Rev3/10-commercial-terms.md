# Commercial Terms

## Pricing summary

| | Option A — mobilisation paid |
|---|---|
| Mobilisation (33% of build value) | AED 5,280 |
| Recurring subscription | AED 1,680/mo |
| Year 1 total | AED 25,440 |

All figures **exclusive of VAT — no VAT is charged** (see below). This is
the only option offered — a zero-mobilisation structure is not available
at this time.

## Payment cadence

Subscription fees are billed **quarterly in advance** — AED 5,040 per
quarter (AED 1,680 × 3).

## VAT

All fees are quoted in AED. SGC TECH AI is not currently registered for
UAE VAT, and no VAT is charged on this proposal.

## VAT gross-up

Should SGC TECH AI become VAT-registered during the term, VAT at the
prevailing rate will be added to invoices issued from the effective date
of registration in accordance with UAE law.

## Financing disclosure

Deferring the build carries an 18% financing component, reflected in the
AED 527/month recovery portion of your subscription fee. Settling the
build in full at kickoff removes this component entirely.

Priced against knowledge layer version **pricing v2.0 (2026-08-03)** —
