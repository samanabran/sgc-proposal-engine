# Commercial Terms

## Pricing summary

| | 24-month term (preferred) | 12-month term |
|---|---|---|
| Mobilisation (at Kickoff) | AED 48,686 | AED 48,686 |
| Recurring subscription | AED 7,790/mo | AED 10,650/mo |
| Billed quarterly in advance | AED 23,370 | AED 31,950 |
| Year 1 total | AED 142,166 | n/a — full term is 12mo |
| Full term commitment | AED 235,646 | AED 176,486 |

**24-month term is recommended** — genuinely lower monthly cost, purely
from spreading the same recovery principal over a longer period (no
discount applied to the recovery component itself). Both options carry
the same mobilisation fee, independent of term choice.

**Monthly billing** is available as an alternative to the standard
quarterly-in-advance cadence, at a **+3% surcharge** (AED 8,020/mo on the
24-month term) — offered specifically to match cash-flow patterns the
Client may already be used to (rent, visas, portal fees), at the cost of
a modest premium for the more frequent billing cycle.

## Payment schedule

Mobilisation (AED 48,686) due at Kickoff. **Refundable in full if the
committed Go-Live date is missed** — a deliberate deviation from the
standard MSA v2026.08 §5.2 position (normally non-refundable except on
Client termination-for-cause), extended here as a downside-protection
concession; SGC TECH AI is already incentivized to hit the 4–6 week
Go-Live target regardless. Security deposit and post-dated cheques per
§09 due prior to Kickoff. Recurring subscription billed quarterly in
advance from the Commencement Date (or monthly, at the surcharge above,
if elected).

## Financing disclosure

> Deferring the build carries an 18% financing component, reflected in
> the recovery portion of your subscription fee (24-month term). Settling
> the build in full at kickoff removes this component entirely.

The 12-month term carries a 6% financing component instead, per
`pricing/policy.yaml: financing_uplift`. This uplift does not vary by
negotiation — only by the term length selected.

## Two-week sandbox

A working sandbox environment is offered for two weeks prior to Kickoff,
at no charge, so the accountability and CRM workflow can be evaluated
before any commitment — before paying AED 48,686 mobilisation, the core
mechanism this proposal is built around can be seen working.
