# Current Process Assessment (As-Is)

| Observed gap | Implied business impact |
|---|---|
| Manual portal posting to Bayut, Property Finder, Dubizzle | Agents/admins upload listings individually; no automated sync; no Meta or Google lead capture |
| No centralized CRM | Lead distribution runs on Zapier automation; reporting entirely in Google Sheets, manually maintained |
| No enforced follow-up mechanism | Root cause flagged directly in discovery: agents receive leads and do not reliably call back; some "give links but they will not immediately call the client" |
| No lead scoring or prioritization | All leads treated equally regardless of source or conversion probability |
| No structured KPI visibility | Management cannot see agent-level performance without manual compilation |

The accountability gap is the primary stated pain point, not a secondary
concern — it was raised as the "root concern" on the original discovery
call, ahead of the portal-integration request itself. Data security and AI
processing concerns were also raised unprompted, indicating any AI-driven
scoring capability (offered separately, see §07) needs to be positioned in
terms of concrete security controls, not general assurances.
