# Partnership Terms

*Several clauses below are marked "pending counsel review" per the
clause-library — this is expected and correct at the internal-draft stage
this document is at. None of these should be treated as final without
that review, regardless of internal sign-off.*

## Term and commencement

> The initial term commences on the date of kickoff, and the first
> invoice — including mobilisation, where applicable — is issued on that
> date. Where kickoff and signature fall on different dates, the term
> commences at kickoff, not at signature.

The initial term is **24 months** (preferred option) or **12 months**
(alternative option — see §10). Following the initial term, this
agreement renews automatically for successive terms of equal length
unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least
thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current term, subject to
Post-Recovery Continuation below.

## Adoption

> The Client nominates a named data-entry owner responsible for day-to-day
> system use. SGC TECH AI checks agreed activity thresholds at day 30
> following go-live. If those thresholds are not met by day 60, SGC TECH
> AI provides one remediation and retraining session at no additional
> charge.

Given the accountability gap is this deal's primary driver, the day-30
check specifically includes callback-logging compliance, not just general
system usage.

## Clawback

> If this subscription is terminated before the end of the committed term
> for any reason other than SGC TECH AI's material breach, the unrecovered
> balance of the implementation value becomes immediately due and payable.

## Post-recovery continuation

> The implementation component is fully recovered by the end of the
> committed term. Thereafter the subscription continues month-to-month at
> the same rate with 30 days' written notice. No reduction in the
> subscription fee is implied.

## Security instruments (elevated risk band)

Per `risk-security-matrix.yaml`, this deal's current risk band (elevated,
placeholder-driven — see `02-calc/risk-assessment.yaml`) requires two
instruments beyond mobilisation:

**Security deposit** *(draft, pending counsel review)*:

> The Client provides a security deposit equal to 2 month(s) of the
> subscription fee prior to kickoff, held by SGC TECH AI for the duration
> of the term. The deposit is applied against any outstanding balance on
> termination and refunded within 30 days of the term's end if no balance
> remains outstanding.

**Post-dated cheques** *(draft, pending counsel review)*:

> The Client provides a set of post-dated cheques covering the deferred
> balance, to be presented only in the event of non-payment under the
> terms of this agreement.

Both instruments are placeholder-driven and should be re-confirmed once
the two unconfirmed risk-assessment inputs (entity age, VAT registration)
are verified — the required instrument set may loosen materially.

## Exclusivity

> This pricing is specific to the scope, user count, and term set out in
> this proposal and is not transferable to other scopes or entities.

## Key person and subcontracting *(draft, pending counsel review)*

> SGC TECH AI assigns qualified personnel to this engagement and reserves
> the right to substitute personnel, including subcontracted specialists,
> at its discretion, provided the substitution does not materially reduce
> the quality of delivery under this agreement.

## IP and configuration *(draft, pending counsel review — LGPL check required, Community edition in scope)*

> The Client owns all of its own data unconditionally, at all times. SGC
> TECH AI retains ownership of the specific configuration and
> customization work built for the Client until the implementation value
> is fully recovered per this agreement's term, subject to the licensing
> terms of any underlying open-source components.

## Dispute resolution and jurisdiction *(draft, pending counsel review)*

> This agreement is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates and
> the regulations of IFZA (Dubai Silicon Oasis), per SGC TECH AI's
> registered licence authority. Any dispute arising under this agreement
> is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Dubai, UAE.

## Liability cap *(draft, pending counsel review — G25, mandatory on every proposal)*

> SGC TECH AI's total liability under this agreement is capped at the
> total fees paid by the Client in the 12 months preceding the claim.
> Neither party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, or
> consequential damages, including loss of profit or business
> interruption.
