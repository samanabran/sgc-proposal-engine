Executive Summary

Kallat Properties runs a 40-agent sales team on manual portal posting to

Bayut, Property Finder, and Dubizzle, with lead distribution via Zapier

automation and pipeline reporting entirely in Google Sheets. The core gap

is not assignment logic — it's follow-through: leads are handed to agents

with no enforced accountability, no callback tracking, and no escalation

when a lead goes cold. This proposal sets out a Subscription Recovery

Model to bring lead management, agent accountability, and reporting into

one Odoo platform, with no separate implementation invoice.

Scope snapshot

Property/unit register, tenancy and contract tracking, TRN-compliant

invoicing, centralized CRM with lead-locking and mandatory callback

logging, agent role/permission setup, reporting dashboards, and migration

of 500 historical records — sized for your full 40-agent team.

Relevant to real estate brokerage

Lead accountability is where generic CRM tools fail brokerages with a

larger agent team — a lead assigned to an agent is only as good as the

follow-through behind it. Our accountability configuration (lead-locking,

mandatory callback logging, automated escalation) is scoped and priced as

its own work package specifically because of this, not folded into a

generic "CRM setup" line that leaves the actual accountability gap

unaddressed.

Future State Vision (To-Be)

The future state replaces fragmented Zapier/Sheets tooling with one

connected operating platform: leads flow into a single pipeline with

source attribution, every lead is locked to its assigned agent with

mandatory callback logging and timestamps, and management gains automated

escalation when an agent fails to act within an SLA window — closing the

accountability gap that was the primary reason the original proposal

stalled.

  • A single lead record per prospect, with property/unit and tenancy

    • context attached.

  • Centralized CRM pipeline replacing Google Sheets as the reporting

    • backbone, with real-time dashboards.

  • Mandatory callback logging and automated escalation on missed

    • follow-through — directly addressing "agents receive leads and do not

    call back."

  • TRN-compliant invoicing tied to the deal/tenancy record.
  • A foundation ready for portal-sync and AI-scoring add-ons (§07) once

    • the core accountability and reporting layer is proven in use.

    This is a phased approach: this proposal's base scope establishes the

    accountability and reporting foundation; portal automation and AI-driven

    lead scoring remain available as separately-priced additions once the

    foundation is live, rather than bundled into a single large build.

Term and commencement

The initial term commences on the date of kickoff, and the first
invoice — including mobilisation, where applicable — is issued on that
date. Where kickoff and signature fall on different dates, the term
commences at kickoff, not at signature.

The initial term is 24 months (preferred option) or 12 months

(alternative option — see §10). Following the initial term, this

agreement renews automatically for successive terms of equal length

unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least

thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current term, subject to

Post-Recovery Continuation below.

Adoption

The Client nominates a named data-entry owner responsible for day-to-day
system use. SGC TECH AI checks agreed activity thresholds at day 30
following go-live. If those thresholds are not met by day 60, SGC TECH
AI provides one remediation and retraining session at no additional
charge.

Given the accountability gap is this deal's primary driver, the day-30

check specifically includes callback-logging compliance, not just general

system usage.

Clawback

If this subscription is terminated before the end of the committed term
for any reason other than SGC TECH AI's material breach, the unrecovered
balance of the implementation value becomes immediately due and payable.

Post-recovery continuation

The implementation component is fully recovered by the end of the
committed term. Thereafter the subscription continues month-to-month at
the same rate with 30 days' written notice. No reduction in the
subscription fee is implied.
